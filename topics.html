Research topics

Two streams connecting organisations and digital decision-making.

My research examines how AI is introduced into organisations and how recommender systems shape individual decisions. Across both streams, I am interested in the design and consequences of digital technologies in practice.

01

Organisations & work

The organizational AI transformation

How organisations build the roles, capabilities and forms of collaboration needed to make AI work in practice.

Generative AI is not only a new tool. Its introduction changes how organisations distribute expertise, coordinate work, support adoption and develop new capabilities.

My research focuses in particular on two related areas: GenAI champions, who help translate AI strategy into local practice, and collaboration in the age of AI, including how generative AI affects teamwork, learning, communication, coordination and trust.

  • How should GenAI champion roles and programmes be designed?
  • How do organisations build capabilities for sustained AI adoption?
  • How does AI change collaboration beyond task efficiency?
02

Digital decision-making

Recommender Systems & AI Agents

How digital recommendation environments influence what people consider, choose and delegate.

Recommender systems shape online decision-making by selecting, ordering and presenting alternatives. With AI agents, these systems increasingly move from suggesting options towards interacting with users and acting on their behalf.

My research examines both conventional recommender systems and agentic interfaces. I am particularly interested in how recommendation design affects cognitive effort, autonomy, choice, decision quality, satisfaction and the broader consumer journey.

  • How do recommender systems shape consumer decision-making?
  • When do AI agents reduce effort without reducing autonomy?
  • How do recommendation design and choice architecture influence decision quality?
