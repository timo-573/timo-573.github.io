Moving Beyond Task Efficiency: How Generative AI Challenges Teamwork

Timo Mayer & Tim-Julian Schwehn

An exploratory organisational case study examining how generative AI affects team inputs, mediators and perceived performance. The findings surface gains in task efficiency alongside challenges for learning, communication and information management.

European Conference on Information Systems (ECIS) 2025 Proceedings, Paper 9.
